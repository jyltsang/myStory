# myStory
A continuous transformation from recycling and community project management into cloud and cybersecurity engineering.


## Hi, I'm John

I build systems that solve real problems. My career didn't start in a computer science lab. It started in environmental charities in Hong Kong, then worked in a philanthropic trust, where I realised that technology is only as good as its implementation. Since 2022, I have been based in Edinburgh, working as a in-house Salesforce adminto develop solution to maximise the productivity for sales and marketers. Now I am transitioning into cybersecurity to build and defend resilient systems for good.

---

### My Journey

* **The Initiative:** While studying for my MBA and working in a philanthropic trust, I noticed my organisation was paying for Salesforce but leaving it completely unused. Instead of sticking to my strict job description as a grantor, I took Salesforce on as a side project. I gathered business requirements, designed the architecture, and drove the implementation. We became probably one of the very first philanthropic trusts in Hong Kong to move beyond a simple database and fully digitalise the entire grant management process by using Salesforce.

* **The Pivot:** Following the Umbrella Revolution and COVID-19, I relocated to Edinburgh in 2022. I took this opportunity to transition into a full-time Salesforce Administrator, driven by a vision to help impact-driven organisations maximise their operational efficiency. 
* **The Technical Awakening:** After 4 years in the Salesforce ecosystem, I realised I wanted to be closer to the metal. While PaaS environments are powerful, they abstract away the underlying infrastructure and limit custom security controls. I wanted to build from scratch. In 2025, I taught myself to code and built CantonLink (https://cantonlink.org/), an AI-powered Cantonese learning web app that offers Yale, Jyutping, and character support, focusing on spoken Cantonese.
* **The New Battlefield:** I love the cat-and-mouse game of attackers and defenders. The constantly changing landscape of cybersecurity is exactly the kind of complex, high-stakes environment I want to operate in. I am now actively bridging my systems administration background with hands-on security engineering.

---

### What I'm Building Now

* **Cloud Infrastructure & Security:** Designing AWS environments, implementing GuardDuty, and automating deployments using Infrastructure as Code.
* **Network & Threat Analysis:** Building segmented home lab environments, deploying Wazuh SIEM for continuous endpoint telemetry, and conducting packet inspection using Wireshark to analyse TCP/TLS traffic.
* **Cryptography & Data Masking:** Leveraging my Apex background to design AES-256 tokenisation modules for sensitive field data.
